# One Timeline Labs Research Vision

> **Technology should never replace musicians.**
>
> **It should help them understand, preserve, and improve their own musical expression.**

---

# About

**One Timeline Labs** is an independent research and development initiative exploring the future of musical performance analysis.

Our objective is not to automate creativity, but to provide musicians with new ways to observe, understand, and preserve their own musical expression.

DrumScore AI is the first platform developed under this vision.

---

# Mission

For centuries, music has been represented as notation.

Modern digital audio introduced waveform visualization.

MIDI introduced event-based performance data.

While these representations are invaluable, they do not fully describe how experienced musicians shape timing, energy, interaction, and expression during performance.

Our mission is to investigate computational methods capable of describing those additional dimensions.

# Hidden Groove

Traditional rhythm analysis primarily evaluates timing accuracy.

However, experienced performers often communicate musical intent through subtle characteristics that extend beyond note placement.

Examples include:

- transient development
- attack characteristics
- dynamic envelope
- micro-timing relationships
- energy distribution
- interaction between simultaneous events

One Timeline Labs refers to this collection of performance characteristics as the **Hidden Groove Layer**.

The purpose of this concept is to encourage measurable research into aspects of musical performance that are widely perceived by musicians but only partially described by existing analytical tools.

---

# Beat Motion Engine

The Beat Motion Engine is a proposed analytical framework for representing musical events as multidimensional motion.

Rather than describing performance only as "early" or "late", the engine aims to characterize each event using multiple observable features, including:

- timing
- transient profile
- attack acceleration
- spectral energy evolution
- temporal interaction
- dynamic behavior

These descriptors may later be extended through additional signal-processing and machine-learning research.

# Long-Term Research

Future investigation may include:

- performer similarity analysis
- ensemble synchronization
- improvisation modeling
- expressive performance visualization
- educational feedback systems
- live performance synchronization
- multimodal musical motion analysis

The architecture intentionally provides extension points for future synchronization with physiological measurements such as heart rate, breathing, motion sensors, and other biosignals for research purposes.

These capabilities are exploratory and are **not intended as medical functionality or therapeutic claims**.

# Philosophy

Music is more than a sequence of notes.

Every performance represents the interaction of time, motion, energy, dynamics, and human intention.

Our goal is not to redefine music, but to develop computational tools that allow musicians and researchers to observe these dimensions with greater clarity.

---

# Long-Term Goal

To establish an open research platform that helps musicians, educators, engineers, and researchers better understand musical performance.

Rather than replacing musicians, we aim to build technologies that preserve and deepen human musical expression.
